Dog Encyclopedia

Below are three popular breeds. Click "Pet me" to say hello!

Beagle

  • Size: Small. Usually 13-15 inches tall and about 20-30 pounds.
  • Personality: Friendly, curious, and cheerful. Beagles love to sniff and explore.
  • Everyday care: Needs daily walks and play. Their floppy ears should be checked and cleaned often.

Golden Retriever

  • Size: Large. Usually 21-24 inches tall and about 55-75 pounds.
  • Personality: Gentle, smart, and eager to please. Great with families and kids.
  • Everyday care: Needs exercise and brushing a few times a week to keep the coat tidy.

Poodle

  • Size: Varies. Standard, Miniature, and Toy sizes all exist.
  • Personality: Clever, lively, and proud. Poodles learn new tricks quickly.
  • Everyday care: Curly coat needs regular grooming. Keep nails trimmed and teeth clean.
